# Client Brief

Me. I'm the client. James Maggs.

The goal is to build a personal site that establishes a long-horizon professional presence. It is not a "hire me" site — I'm not actively job-seeking. It's a place that communicates who I am, where I've been, and how I think about what's coming next in software engineering.

The site should be visually distinctive and work across devices.

## Objectives

- Build a professional presence that compounds over time.
- Communicate a clear point of view on where software engineering is going.
- Attract sharp technical peers, thoughtful collaborators, and interesting conversations.
- Be a site I'm proud to share as a reflection of my thinking and craft.
- Keep recruiters and potential employers in scope — but stop optimising for them first.

## Target Audience

Primary: Sharp technical peers — engineers, leaders, and builders who are serious about where the industry is going.

Secondary: Recruiters and potential employers who encounter James through referral, LinkedIn, or a CV.

## What Success Looks Like

A visitor leaves thinking: *"This person has seen it all, knows what's real, and I want to talk to them."*

Not: *"This person is available and has the right skills."*
