# Client Brief

Me. I’m the client. James Maggs.

The goal is to design and build a personal portfolio website that showcases my work as a software engineer, manager and leader. The content should be positioned such that it presents my suitability for the future needs of software engineering rather than focusing on the past.

The site should be visually appealing and responsive, working on multiple devices.

## Objectives

- Attract recruitment agents, former colleagues or potential employers.
- Highlight my skills and experience.
- Focus on skills for the future: agentic programming.
- Differentiate me from other candidates.
- Make contacting me easy and automatic.

## Target Audience

- Recruiters
- Potential Employers
- Former Colleagues
